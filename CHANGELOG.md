## [0.2026.1] - 2026-10-20

- Run on macOS 13 or later, Windows and Linux.
- Show Home Assistant devices in a menu bar or tray panel as tiles by room, lit while they are on.
- Switch a device from its tile, and open its page from the tile's arrow.
- Dim, color, move, play, lock or start a device from its page, with presets for a light's colors and a cover's position.
- Control an air conditioner's power, temperature, mode, fan speed, airflow, Eco Mode and Sleep.
- Connect to Home Assistant from Settings in the panel with its address and a long-lived access token.
- Open NeoHM at login when turned on in Settings.
- Show the app in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese or Korean, following the system or the language chosen in Settings.
- Check for new versions daily or from Settings, notify you of each and install them in place.
